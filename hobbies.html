Hobbies

Writing

I write fiction in both Urdu and English, and have been doing it since 2019, starting with short stories in Urdu when I was at Cadet College Hasan Abdal. I post most of it on my Substack, haqiq. I also worked as a script writer for the NUST Drama Club and was part of its Literary Society.

Reading

I read a mix of fiction and non-fiction, and when I don't have time to sit and read I listen to audiobooks instead, usually while walking around campus or during commutes.

Field Hockey

I play field hockey when I get the chance. It's less regular than my writing, but it's a good way to take a break from screens and assignments.

Game Development

For one of my university courses I built a small game in Unreal Engine, which got me interested in the technical side of game design as well.
